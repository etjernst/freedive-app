Winnow Logo

A first vector direction for a training capture and coaching app: quiet, precise, and useful beyond one athlete. The name frames the product as a way to separate signal from noise, while the mark keeps a subtle water and breath reference.

Winnowing signal The repeated curves narrow toward a single centerline: plans, sensations, and training logs becoming usable judgment.
Still aquatic The palette and wave rhythm keep the freediving context, but avoid depth imagery so the app can travel beyond private use.
Quiet coaching The orange ticks hint at reps and targets; the centered line keeps the brand aligned with “No quick decisions.”
