Winnow True Negative-Space Options

Ten proper negative-space variants. In each icon, the field is drawn first and the W is removed from it with a mask. The W exists only as background showing through the sifted field.

Option 61

Particle Disk

A dense sifted disk with the W removed. This is the most literal correction: no W stroke remains, just the paper-colored absence through the particle field.

Option 62

Grain Wreath Cutout

Closer to the blob-and-dot reference, but with a real W-shaped hole. The field is looser, so the W is more delicate and depends on enough grains around it.

Option 63

Scan Field Cutout

The W is subtracted from ordered scan lines. This connects to the data-sifting reference and should scale better than a field made only of dots.

Option 64

Soft Square

A PWA-friendly tile with the W knocked out. This is less airy, but it is probably the most practical if the icon must survive Android home-screen cropping.

Option 65

Half Mass

A more organic sifted body with the W cut out of it. This is warmer than the scan-line versions, but it needs careful refinement to avoid looking blobby.

Option 66

Fine Point Cloud

The field is only points, with the W removed from the cloud. It is conceptually clean, but probably needs larger points or a denser cloud for small sizes.

Option 67

Dense Pebble Cutout

The chunky version of the reference. Because the pieces are large, the W reads more clearly as a cutout, though the mark becomes more illustrative.

Option 68

Monocolor Field

The cleanest production candidate: one color, one cutout. If this works visually, it will be easiest to turn into favicons, masks, and monochrome assets.

Option 69

Offset Field

The W cutout is shifted right inside an irregular field. This avoids perfect badge symmetry while still keeping the negative-space rule honest.

Option 70

Layered Sift

A hybrid of particles and linework, with the W knocked out of the layered field. It is more complex, but it connects the two reference families.
