Winnow Full-Name Logo Options

Ten options in the lane of the reference: the full name is the main identity, with a small companion mark that behaves like grains, observations, or selected signals. The mark supports the word rather than competing with it.

Option 21

Dispersed Grain

The closest relative of the reference. The dot field is deliberately loose, while the wordmark does the serious work. This feels quiet and editorial rather than techy.

Option 22

Settled Grain

The grains have a gentle downward rhythm, like noisy inputs settling before a decision. The small baseline curve is subordinate and should read as grounding, not a wave.

Option 23

Grain Over The I's

The companion mark moves inside the name by making the i-dots part of the identity. It is still more than only a name, but much more integrated than a separate icon.

Option 24

Selected O

The o becomes the place where one signal is noticed. It is slightly more distinctive as an app icon source, because the marked o can be lifted out as a small standalone badge.

Option 25

W As Vessel

The doubled trough shape of the W gets the grains, which makes the name itself carry the winnowing metaphor. This is one of the more elegant routes if we customize the W later.

Option 26

Marginal Notes

A small note-card mark beside the word. This leans into training journal and coaching read, making Winnow feel like a thoughtful app rather than an abstract data brand.

Option 27

Lowercase Companion

A compact lowercase w serves as the app-icon seed while the full wordmark remains formal. It feels personal and less corporate than a boxed monogram.

Option 28

After The Word

The grain field comes after Winnow, as if the app produces the filtered read. This keeps the left edge clean for headers and makes the mark feel less like a decorative logo bug.

Option 29

Quiet Sans

A less literary version of the reference. The sans wordmark makes the app feel more contemporary, while the dot cluster preserves the hand-sorted, human quality.

Option 30

Fine Grain

A smaller, more delicate version of the reference. It might be the best lane if the brand should feel understated: the visual mark is texture, not a symbol trying too hard.
