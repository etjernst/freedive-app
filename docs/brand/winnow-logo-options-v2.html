Winnow Logo Options V2

Ten new directions. I avoided compass forms, ripple-W gestures, centered vertical bodies, cigarette-like line-and-dot marks, and overly symmetric compositions. These lean more product-brand than sport-brand.

Option 11

Sorted Field

A structured field where one point has emerged as useful. It says filtering and training data without implying motion, smoke, diving depth, or a mascot.

Option 12

Open Aperture

A less perfect cycle: open, off-center, and observational. It keeps the decent parts of the breath-cycle idea while moving away from smartwatch symmetry.

Option 13

Wordmark With Notch

Mostly a wordmark, with a small custom winnowing notch tucked under the double-n. This is restrained and grown-up, and probably the easiest to live with daily.

Option 14

Marked Log

A capture/logbook mark with a diagonal annotation. It is more product UI than logo-poem: good if Winnow should feel like a tool first and a brand second.

Option 15

Decision Card

Two candidate readings and a deliberate next step. It hints at planning and coaching without becoming a generic graph or a literal checklist.

Option 16

Asymmetric Weave

A softer, less mechanical version of trend lines. The forms do not overlap or resolve too neatly, so it avoids the "mistake" feeling of the earlier narrowing path.

Option 17

Selected Grain

A small field of observations with one selected. This is the less cigarette-like cousin of the dots idea: no horizontal baseline, no smoke silhouette.

Option 18

Bounded Read

Brackets around the signal: Winnow as the place where messy training gets held, compared, and interpreted. It is compact and app-icon friendly.

Option 19

Cut Plane

A diagonal filter plane, like separating layers of evidence. It is intentionally not centered and not aquatic; more analytical, a little sharper.

Option 20

Lowercase Badge

A gentle monogram, more journal than smartwatch. The lowercase w makes Winnow feel personal and calm, while the small dot keeps the idea of a found signal.
