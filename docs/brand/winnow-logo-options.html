Winnow Logo Options

Ten deliberately different directions for Winnow. These avoid the vertical-body symmetry that made the previous mark read as an insect, and explore the name as filtering, noticing, composure, and training signal.

Option 1

Quiet Wordmark

A typography-led identity with only a small ripple underline. This is the most app-store and public-product friendly direction: calm, mature, and hard to misread.

Option 2

Signal From Noise

Scattered inputs settle into one clear signal. This leans into the word Winnow directly, and feels broader than freediving: useful for coaching, training, notes, and analysis.

Option 3

Ripple W

A single soft W made from a waterline. It keeps the aquatic reference but makes the letterform primary, so it avoids looking like a sport logo or a literal dive symbol.

Option 4

The Sieve

A more literal winnowing metaphor: many inputs above, one useful output below. This has a quietly practical feel and could work well if the app becomes a training-analysis tool for others.

Option 5

Composure Compass

Less about filtering, more about orientation under pressure. It says "notice, decide, stay centered," which fits the mantra and the coaching dimension of the app.

Option 6

Training Log

A practical notebook mark. It is less poetic, but very clear: Winnow captures structured sessions and turns them into something readable. Good if usability should lead the brand.

Option 7

Narrowing Path

Three bands compress into a final useful point. It suggests recent sessions, capacity trends, and coaching interpretation without using charts or literal sport imagery.

Option 8

Breath Cycle

A circular mark for training cycles, breath cycles, and review loops. It is the most physiological of the set while still staying abstract enough for a general coaching app.

Option 9

Monogram W

The safest app-icon route: a strong W with a small signal detail. It would scale cleanly, feel premium, and avoid overexplaining what the app does.

Option 10

No Quick Decisions

A slow path crossing three possible readings before settling. This is the most mantra-driven option: it visually says do not react to the first signal, wait for the clearer one.
