Winnow Converging Points Options

Ten variants combining converging paths with dotted observations. The dots make the forms feel like data points rather than streams, while the convergence keeps the Winnow idea: separate, align, read.

Option 51

Dotted Convergence

The most direct blend of the two references: paths converge, but the visible evidence is the individual observations sitting on them.

Option 52

Converging W

The implied paths form a W and converge at the center. It is more letter-driven, and the dots keep it from becoming just a stylized stroke.

Option 53

Resolved Band

Messy, dotted inputs become a stable set of bands. It is the most like the first reference, but with the dots making it feel observational instead of stream-like.

Option 54

Word As Resolution

The observations converge into the word itself instead of into a separate mark. This is wordmark-first and could feel elegant in a header.

Option 55

Dotted Fan

A broader fan of observations narrows into three stable readings. It is more data-point-heavy and less letter-driven, but it has a clean Winnow metaphor.

Option 56

W At The Merge

The W appears at the point where the observations start to resolve. This makes the name’s initial letter the outcome, not an unrelated decoration.

Option 57

Sparse Field

A more restrained version with fewer points. This avoids the map/spaghetti problem and may be easier to make elegant with careful spacing.

Option 58

App Tile

The concept contained in a soft rounded tile for PWA use. Less airy than the loose marks, but it solves the home-screen icon problem directly.

Option 59

Dotted Word Path

A lighter cousin of option 54: dots converge into the full word. It is probably better for a header lockup than a standalone app icon.

Option 60

Final W Read

The observations converge into a clean W mark. This is more brandable than the pure line studies because it gives the app a memorable initial glyph.
