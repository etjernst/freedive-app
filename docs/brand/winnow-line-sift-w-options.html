Winnow Line-Sift W Options

Ten variants based on the data-sifting reference: a noisy mass meeting ordered lines. Each option integrates a W through negative space, line interruptions, or the boundary between texture and structure.

Option 41

Negative W Lines

The scan lines carry a W-shaped absence. This keeps the reference intact while making the letter appear as the thing the ordered read reveals.

Option 42

W Boundary

The edge between mass and ordered lines is shaped like a W. It is subtler than a drawn letter, but more integrated with the sifting metaphor.

Option 43

Striped W

The W is the interruption in the ordered data field. This is the boldest and clearest letterform, though it risks feeling more graphic-design-poster than app icon.

Option 44

Half-Dust W

A direct W mark with dust on the unsorted side and lines on the sorted side. It is less elegant, but very readable and easy to translate into a small icon.

Option 45

Lowercase Sift

A warmer route: the lowercase w sits inside the sifted mass while the lines pass behind it. This feels less technical and more like a training journal.

Option 46

Line-Cut Vessel

The W is implied by the trough carved through a sifted body. It is less literal than the uppercase W options and ties back to the vessel idea.

Option 47

W Aperture

The reference image, but with a W-shaped aperture interrupting the scan field. This is the most faithful to the supplied concept and probably worth refining.

Option 48

Monoline Winnow

A lighter W stroke laid across the line field. It is the least emblematic option and could work well in a header, though the app icon may need a bolder version.

Option 49

Compact App Tile

A contained version designed for PWA icons. It gives up some airy elegance, but it would be easier to render consistently across splash screens and home screens.

Option 50

Full Word Scan

The W is treated as the sifted mass and the full name anchors the lockup below. This is simple, maybe too simple, but much less likely to become a confusing abstract symbol.
