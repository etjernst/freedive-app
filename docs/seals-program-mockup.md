# Seals pool program, 12-week mockup

A first draft to react to, not a finished program.
Two pool sessions a week, a rest day before each, no strength or static work in this version.
Everything is DYNB (bifins) except the tortuga, which is its own thing.
No packing anywhere, no max attempts, and every distance can be turned down to 25 m for newer members.
Distances assume a 25 m pool and a member who swims around 75 m DYNB.

The order inside a pool session never changes: the hardest apnea first, sprints or intervals second, the hypercapnic swim third, an easy 100 m recovery swim last.

## The four phases

Base (weeks 1–4) is CO2 tolerance: short-rest sets, pyramids, hypercapnic swimming.
Build (weeks 5–8) adds speed and the first submax dives.
Specialization (weeks 9–12) is submax dives at a rising percentage of PB plus an endurance ladder.
Taper (weeks 11–12, inside specialization) keeps the same sessions and cuts the number of intervals week on week while intensity holds.

Each exercise in the app will have a phase tag (base, build, specialization, taper) and a capacity tag (CO2, O2, lactic, technique, mental), and its reps and rests fill in from the phase you pick.

## Weekly layout

| Session | Base (1–4) | Build (5–8) | Specialization (9–12) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pool 1 | fixed-rest set, pyramid, hypercapnic swim | submax dive, dolphin sprints, hypercapnic swim | submax dive, endurance ladder, tortuga |
| Pool 2 | over-under warm-up, sprints, hypercapnic swim | over-under warm-up, longer sprints, hypercapnic swim | fixed-rest set, tortuga, hypercapnic swim |

## The exercises

Fixed-rest set (CO2; base, specialization, taper).
Repeated 50 m DYNB at your normal dive pace with a rest cap.
Base: 4, 5, 6, 7 reps across the four weeks, rest cap 60 s.
Specialization: 6 reps, rest cap 45 s.
Taper: 5 then 4 reps, cap 45 s.

Pyramid (CO2; base).
50-25-25-50 in weeks 1–2, 50-50-25-50-50 in weeks 3–4, rest no longer than 60 s and shorter when you can.

Hypercapnic surface swim (CO2; every phase).
Freestyle on the surface in short fins, no wetsuit, breathing a fixed 3 times per 25 m.
100, 150, 200, 250 m in base; 300–350 m in build; 400 m in specialization; 300 then 200 m in the taper.

Recovery swim (every phase).
100 m any style, relaxed, breathing normally, to close every session.

Over-under warm-up (technique; base, build).
100 m as 25 m freestyle on the surface, turn, 25 m DYNB underwater without stopping, twice.

Sprints (lactic; base, build).
Base: 2 sets of 6 x 25 m all-out DYNB, 20 s between reps, 2 min between sets, in weeks 1–2; 6 x 50 m with 60 s in weeks 3–4.
Build: 5–6 x 75 m with 75–120 s rest.

Dolphin sprints (lactic; build).
Each rep is 25 m of all-out dolphin kick on the surface (snorkel, or on your back so you can breathe), then a duck dive straight into 10 m underwater at sprint pace.
2 sets of 4 in weeks 5–6, 2 sets of 5 in weeks 7–8, 60–90 s between reps, 3 min between sets, short fins, no wetsuit.

Submax dive (O2, mental; build, specialization, taper).
One dive with no warm-up and no breathe-up, breathing normally beforehand, face dry until you go.
Aim for at least the target percentage of your PB with no upper limit, but surface before your limit and never finish shaky.
Build: 65% of PB in weeks 6 and 8.
Specialization: 70–75% every week.
Taper: 80%.

Endurance ladder (CO2, volume; specialization).
Mixed 75 m and 50 m DYNB with rest no longer than the dive time.
1 x 75 + 5 x 50 in week 9, then convert one 50 into a 75 each week: 2 + 4, 3 + 3, 4 + 2.

Tortuga (mental, CO2; build, specialization).
Crawl along the bottom as slowly as you can, covering at most 50 m in the longest possible time; no goggles so you do not watch the clock.
Enters in week 5 as a single slow crawl after the sprints and stays in every week from then on.

## Week by week, in one line each

Weeks 1–4: pool 1 is fixed-rest set (4, 5, 6, 7 x 50), pyramid, hypercapnic swim (100 to 250 m), recovery swim; pool 2 is over-under, sprints, the same hypercapnic swim, recovery swim.
Weeks 5–8: pool 1 is submax (weeks 6 and 8), dolphin sprints, hypercapnic swim (300 to 350 m), recovery swim; pool 2 is over-under, 75 m sprints, hypercapnic swim, tortuga, recovery swim.
Weeks 9–10: pool 1 is submax at 70–75%, endurance ladder, tortuga, recovery swim; pool 2 is 6 x 50 fixed-rest at 45 s, tortuga, 400 m hypercapnic swim, recovery swim.
Weeks 11–12: the same two sessions with the submax at 80%, the fixed-rest set down to 5 then 4 reps, the ladder continuing, and the swim down to 300 then 200 m.

## Things I would like your view on

Whether 50 m is the right default for the fixed-rest set or it should start at 25 m.
Whether 12 weeks is right, or the same shape over 16 weeks.
Whether the dolphin sprints and the endurance ladder are things you would run with the club.
When to bring static in, and whether a reduced-lung table belongs in a club program at all.
