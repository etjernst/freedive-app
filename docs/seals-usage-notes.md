Winnow Seals: https://emiliatjernstrom.com/winnow-seals/

- Open the link on your phone and, if you like, add it to your home screen (Share, then "Add to Home Screen" on iPhone; the three-dot menu, then "Add to Home screen" on Android). It then opens like an app and works offline. Everything you enter stays on your phone; nothing is uploaded anywhere.
- The home screen has two things: the buttons at the top (New session, Open sessions) and the exercise library below. Tap any exercise in the library to expand it and see the sets, reps and rests it comes with; the button inside the expanded card starts a session with that exercise already in it.
- Above the library, the bar with Base / Build / Specialization / Taper filters exercises by training phase and, more importantly, sets the reps and rests that get filled in when you add an exercise, since most exercises change across the cycle (the fixed-rest set is 4 x 50 m with 60 s in base but 6 x 50 m with 45 s in specialization). Below that, Discipline and Trains are plain filters: tap a chip to narrow the list, tap it again to clear.
- Sessions is the list of everything you have built or logged; tap one to reopen it, the small x deletes it. New sessions autosave as you go, so you can leave and come back.
- Settings has two sets of numbers: your own personal bests and paces, and a "template student" (a made-up 100 m DYNB diver at 25 s per 25 m, and so on). The session estimate can run off either; the template student is there so you can time a session for the group before anyone has entered their own numbers. Change those numbers to whatever fits the club.
- Backup on the home screen exports everything to a file and restores from one; worth doing occasionally, since the data live only on the phone.

Example 1: build and time a base-phase pool session
1. Tap New session. Pool length is 25 m by default; add a note for the day if you want.
2. Tap Base in the phase bar. The library shrinks to the base exercises.
3. Tap + on Over-under warm-up, Fixed-rest set, Pyramid, Hypercapnic surface swim, and Recovery swim. Each lands in the session with its base-phase reps and rests already filled in.
4. Tap Hide library. Each exercise shows its reps; tap into any number to change it (say 5 reps instead of 4, or 25 m instead of 50 m).
5. At the bottom, "Estimated session" is the total time. Tap "template student" next to "Estimate for" to time it against the template diver rather than your own PBs. Every exercise also shows its own "Est." line, so you can see where the time goes.
6. That is it; it is saved. Find it again under Sessions.

Example 2: log what actually happened
1. Open the session under Sessions and tap "Log actuals" at the bottom.
2. For each exercise, enter what was done: distance, time, how the recoveries felt. Reps that went as planned only need a tap.
3. Tap Done. The session now shows as logged in the list, and the numbers feed the Insights view over time.
